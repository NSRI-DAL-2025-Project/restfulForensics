Introduction

restful forensics is an open-source platform for forensic genetics research workflow and method validation. This tool is built as a Shiny app in R and incorporates commonly used packages, functions, and software for genetic data preparation, pre-processing, and exploratory data analysis.

restful forensics is developed at the Natural Sciences Research Institute, University of the Philippines Diliman, Quezon City.
